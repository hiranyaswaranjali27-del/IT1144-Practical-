Study Tips

⏰ Time Management

  • Create a study timetable.
  • Set daily study goals.
  • Take short breaks every hour.

📚 Exam Preparation

  • Practice past papers regularly.
  • Review notes weekly.
  • Focus on weak subjects.

🧠 Memory Techniques

  • Use flashcards.
  • Create mind maps.
  • Teach concepts to a friend.

🌱 Healthy Study Habits

  • Get enough sleep.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Keep your study area clean.
